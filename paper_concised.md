***Introduction***

China’s stock market has climbed to a record high of more than USD 10 trillion, making it the second-largest in the world after the US.

However, China’s stock market has significant differences:
The Chinese stock market is dominated by retail investors (99.8%), which tends to result in increased turnover.
However, it is noticeable that China’s financial system is centrally controlled (SOE), bank-dominated, and uniquely relation-ship driven.
The Chinese market has limited history of short sales (since it was restricted until 2010), which makes long-only portfolios more suitable for future analysis.

In the last millennium, China has been experiencing a highly dynamic development, which included expenditure of its capital markets’ openness. 

This makes China a great target for the implementation of machine learning techniques for future analysis in the field of empirical asset pricing. 





***Problem***

There is no large data base of factor returns available for Chinese market.

The paper attempts to build a unique and comprehensive set of factors for empirical asset pricing.
